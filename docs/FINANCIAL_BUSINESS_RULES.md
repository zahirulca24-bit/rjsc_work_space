# ZA Corporate Desk — Financial Business Rules
Version: 1.0 | Date: 2026-10-12 | Status: Proposed detailed rules; policy direction approved

## Approved direction
**Service-wise flexible billing:** Package, Itemized or Hybrid by work. **Internal job costing mandatory for every work**, regardless of client invoice presentation. One work may have multiple forms, invoices, receipts and cost entries. Track client-wide as well as work-level results.

## Financial records and relationships
- Client; Work; Estimate/Budget; Expense/Disbursement; Supplier/Payee; Staff Advance and Settlement; Invoice and Line; Receipt and Allocation; Payment; Cash/Bank Account; Government Fee; Voucher; Adjustment; Approval; Supporting Document; Audit Event.
- Unique immutable voucher/reference; client/work association where applicable; created/approved/posted dates and users; evidence; reversals rather than silent deletion of posted entries.
- Each entry must distinguish cash movement from expense recognition, recoverable pass-through, liability and revenue.

## Billing and collection
- Work-level package/itemized/hybrid rate; professional fees and client-billable disbursements distinguished; tax shown according to verified treatment.
- Partial and multiple invoices/receipts, advances, discounts, credit notes, refunds and write-offs with authority.
- Outstanding = posted invoices less applied receipts and credit adjustments; unapplied client advances tracked separately.
- Client advance is not automatically income; invoice, collection and profit are distinct.

## Costing
- Estimated versus actual; category, date, vendor/payee, paid by, method, receipt, approval, client/work, billable/recoverable status.
- Categories: government fee, government VAT/tax, bank/payment charge, staff direct labor, printing, travel/conveyance, account recovery, authorized third-party service, miscellaneous and allocated overhead.
- A fee included in an account-recovery package must not also be charged as a second government-fee expense.
- Miscellaneous requires reason and evidence. Unverified/irregular or potentially unlawful payments must be flagged for compliance review, not normalized or automatically approved as deductible business expense.

## Example annual-return outlay — ILLUSTRATIVE ONLY, NOT VERIFIED RJSC RATES
Government fee BDT 200; related VAT BDT 30; staff form preparation BDT 50; print BDT 20; miscellaneous BDT 500; transport BDT 100; account recovery BDT 750 (user estimated BDT 500–1,000). **Total example outlay BDT 1,650.** Distinguish reimbursable government disbursements from actual firm expense and avoid double counting. Actual fees, taxes, receipts and recoverability require validation.

## Cash, bank, government fees
- Cash Book, Bank Book, Government Fee Register; opening balance, voucher, date, account, reference, payer/payee, reconciliation.
- Cash-to-bank deposit and internal transfers are neither revenue nor expense.
- Government fee register: assessed type/amount, payment reference, challan/receipt, payment channel, client/work, reconciliation and recovery status.
- Reconcile bank statements and petty cash; segregate payment initiation, approval and reconciliation when feasible.

## Staff advance, reimbursements and payroll
- Staff advance is a receivable/asset until supported settlement; not full expense at disbursement.
- Example: advance BDT 1,000, supported costs BDT 850, balance return/adjustment BDT 150.
- Expense claims may span works but must allocate line by line.
- Staff remuneration/direct labor and fixed payroll require policy; do not count the same salary twice in job and office totals.
- **Staff compensation policy not yet decided:** salary-only / per-work / both.

## Profit reporting
- Job contribution = recognized service revenue minus recognized direct job costs (consistent treatment of pass-through items).
- Work management net profit = contribution minus allocated overhead.
- Client profitability = consolidated results across client works, without double counting.
- Office profit = recognized revenue minus recognized expenses, with applicable depreciation, finance costs, taxes and adjustments.
- Show invoiced, collected, uncollected, advances, payables, recoverable disbursements, direct outlay, contribution and net profit separately.
- Set documented overhead allocation basis before showing authoritative net job profit.

## Financial closing
Operational completion can precede financial close. Financial close requires posted/verified expenses, staff settlement, fee reconciliation, invoices, receipt allocations, outstanding or authorized write-off, and manager/accounts signoff. No destructive deletion of posted records.

## Access & approval
Role-based approvals for work budget, exceptional costs, staff advance/settlement, invoices, discounts, write-offs, bank reconciliation and financial close. Numeric limits are **TBD**. Keep audit trail, supporting evidence and correction history.

## Account recovery costing
Client consent, authorized recovery process, itemized official fee/third-party cost/staff/transport, verified proof, no duplicate expense allocation, encrypted credential vault (separate from accounting exports).

## Outstanding accounting decisions
Entity accounting basis; recognition rules for reimbursed disbursements; invoice VAT/tax handling; government fee classifications; salary allocation; overhead method; approval limits; chart of accounts; fiscal period and reporting format. These must be finalized with the firm's accountant and current Bangladesh rules before implementation.
